## 2. Communication Standards
**Do:** warm/concise, customer's first name, conversational, empathetic, direct
**Don't:** "Hi Customer", excessive exclamations, "We're so excited!", over-apologize, jargon

**Opening:** "Hi [Name]," + "Thanks for reaching out!"
**Closing (always):** "Please let me know if you have any other questions, we're always happy to help! Cheers, [Name], Wamsutta Support"

**Email hygiene:** Always delete CAUTION banner before sending.

**Timeframes (exact phrases):**
- Shipping: "within 1-2 business days"
- Refunds: "within 3-5 business days"
- Never: "by tomorrow", "ASAP", "immediately", "right away"

**Phrase swaps:**
- "at this time" → "currently"
- "pass off" → "drop off"
- Avoid "We apologize for any inconvenience"

**Ticket closure:** Always be the last response. Acknowledge customer's "thank you" before closing.

## 6. Refunds vs Replacements

**Full refund (customer keeps product):**
- Quality disappointment vs legacy BBB
- Persistent chemical smell after washing
- Sheets too thin/flimsy vs price expectations
- Manufacturing defects, washed but defective

**Replacement (customer keeps defective):**
- Defects (tears, holes, discoloration, loose threads)
- Wrong item received (our error)
- Missing items
- FedEx delivery issues (one-time courtesy)
- Legacy customer goodwill

**Ask for return:** changed mind, unopened, no defect

### Shopify Notes Protocol (REQUIRED on every replacement)
- New order: "Replacement order - [reason]"
- New order internal: "Replacement for #[orig]. [context]"
- Original order: "Do not refund — replacement order #[new] processed due to [reason]"
- Partial refunds: note amount + new order #

## 7. Quality Issues

- Percale wrinkling: educate (naturally wrinkles, softens 2-3 washes), full refund, customer keeps
- **Recurring defects (2+):** escalate to supervisor, full refund + final replacement, flag SKU

## 17. Care Instructions
- **Sheets:** cold/warm wash, tumble dry low, no softener, no bleach
- **Sateen:** shake before drying, put on bed immediately
- **Percale:** no hot water, no softener, takes 2-3 washes to soften
- **Towels:** wash before first use, warm, low-med dry, no softener, separately
- **Pillows:** remove zip cover, spot clean insert, fluff regularly
